Before you rely on this. The projection assumes you spend what you entered, keep saving what you entered until you retire, and owe nothing you left out. Add every loan other than your mortgage with “+ Add a loan”. Add a card balance you carry month to month there too, as a credit card, with what you pay on it: paid at the minimum, a card can take decades to clear and cost more in interest than it owed. Retiring early leaves little room for slippage.
