### oas: Old Age Security pension: 75 plus step and recommended amount

The OAS inputs stay: enter the 65 to 74 amount in todays dollars (2026 maximum 762.50 a month, recommended default; prorate for partial residence; raise it yourself for a deferral, 0.6 percent per month up to 36 percent at 70). The planner applies the 10 percent increase from age 75 (ESDC maximum 838.75; the ratio 75 plus to 65 to 74 is exactly 1.10 in every quarter checked since July 2022 and 1.00 before). Quarterly CPI indexation is approximated by annual plan inflation.

```json
{
 "recommendedMonthly": 762.5,
 "ageFactors": [
  {
   "from": 75,
   "factor": 1.1
  }
 ]
}
```
