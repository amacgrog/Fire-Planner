# 7L benefit catalog: for independent verification

Purpose: a list of the income-tested benefits that Canada's planner may model in 7L, with what is
believed about each and what must be confirmed. **Nothing here has been verified on a primary
page** unless it says P. Everything else comes from search-result summaries (S) and must be
checked against the government source before it goes into the code. Retrieved 2026-10-05.

How to use: for each row, find the program's official page (Canada.ca/CRA, ESDC, the province),
confirm or correct the figure, and give the URL and the page's "updated" date. Flag any program
that no longer exists or changed its name.

Modeling constraints (owner): no new inputs; same-year income stands in for the real prior-year
test; benefits are tax-free payments to the household. Inputs available: each partner's age, net
income, the children list (birth years, student flag), province, rent, property tax, couple or
single.
