## Notes

- **AK**: Alaska levies no individual income tax on wages, pensions, retirement withdrawals, Social Security or investment gains, so no state income tax is modeled.
- **CA**: 2025 figures (CalCPA Fast Tax Facts, December 2025; secondary). These are not 2026 figures: the FTB's 2026 schedule was not published at the time of writing, so the 2025 amounts stand for the first plan year and the plan index factor takes them from there. The 1% mental health surcharge over $1 million is its own addition and does not index (the brackets, standard deduction and credits do). Exemption credits are reduced $6 each per $2,500 of federal AGI over the threshold; the 65+ credit is $153 per filer. Not modeled: CalEITC, the Young Child Tax Credit, the senior head of household credit, the joint custody HoH credit; the renter's credit assumes a full year of renting.
- **CT**: Tax tables from Form CT-1040 TCS (Rev. 12/25, tax year 2025, primary): brackets, personal exemption (reduced $1,000 per $1,000 of AGI over $30,000 / $48,000 / $38,000), the 2% add-back, the recapture and the personal tax credit decimals; none is indexed. Retirement rules from OLR report 2025-R-0152 (Sept. 2025): Social Security is exempt under $75,000 AGI ($100,000 joint or head of household), above that no more than 25% of benefits is taxed; pension, annuity and IRA income is fully exempt under $75,000 ($100,000 joint) and phases down to nothing at $100,000 ($150,000 joint), with the IRA deduction at 100% from 2026. Property tax credit up to $300 for the home, 15% less per $10,000 of AGI over $49,500 / $70,500 / $54,500 (secondary; the vehicle tax part is not modeled). The 2026 table revisions, if any, and the CT EITC are not modeled.
- **FL**: Florida levies no individual income tax on wages, pensions, retirement withdrawals, Social Security or investment gains, so no state income tax is modeled.
- **NV**: Nevada levies no individual income tax on wages, pensions, retirement withdrawals, Social Security or investment gains, so no state income tax is modeled.
- **NH**: New Hampshire repealed its Interest and Dividends Tax (3%) for tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025 (HB 2, 2023), and has no tax on wages, pensions or gains, so no state income tax is modeled. Its business taxes are outside this planner.
- **NY**: Bracket thresholds are statutory and not indexed (IT-201-I 2024 and 2025, primary); the 2026 rates 3.9 / 4.4 / 5.15 / 5.4 / 5.9 percent are from secondary sources, as are the 2026 standard deductions ($8,000 / $16,050 / $11,200, unchanged for years) and the child credit. Tax benefit recapture follows the IT-201-I tax computation worksheets (checked against the 2024 constants). $20,000 per filer pension and annuity exclusion from age 59 and a half (traditional withdrawals included). Empire State child credit: $1,000 under age 4 and $500 ages 4-16 for 2026, fully refundable, reduced $16.50 per $1,000 of federal AGI over $75,000 ($110,000 joint), the reduction taken once from the total credit. Not modeled: household credit, real property tax credit, EITC, NYC and Yonkers taxes.
- **PA**: Flat 3.07% with no standard deduction or exemption (pa.gov). Social Security is not taxed. Pension income is excluded; traditional (IRA and 401(k)) withdrawals are excluded only from age 59 and a half per filer, and taxed before it (secondary sources: distributions before 59 and a half are taxable, with cost recovery). Not modeled: the 401(k)-after-retirement exception, Roth cost recovery, the property tax / rent rebate (a paid program). Tax Forgiveness: 100% of the tax at or under $6,500 of eligibility income ($13,000 married) plus $9,500 per dependent, then 10% less per $250 (pa.gov tables); eligibility income is taken as the PA base (qualified retirement payments and Social Security are excluded from it).
- **SD**: South Dakota levies no individual income tax on wages, pensions, retirement withdrawals, Social Security or investment gains, so no state income tax is modeled.
- **TN**: Tennessee repealed the Hall income tax on interest and dividends for tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2021 (Department of Revenue notice HIT-3), and has no tax on wages, pensions or gains, so no state income tax is modeled.
- **TX**: No individual income tax (Texas Constitution art. VIII, sec. 24-a). The Comptroller's list of the 100 taxes and fees it administers has no income tax; the constitutional text was not opened.
- **WA**: A tax on long-term capital gains only (real estate and retirement accounts excluded; the engine's gains are brokerage gains). 7% on the first $1 million above the $278,000 standard deduction (the same for a couple), 9.9% above (ESSB 5813, 2025; DOR). The standard deduction is indexed for inflation; the $1 million tier is not. No wage or retirement income tax.
- **WY**: Wyoming levies no individual income tax on wages, pensions, retirement withdrawals, Social Security or investment gains, so no state income tax is modeled.
