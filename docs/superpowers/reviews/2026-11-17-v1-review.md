This was a post-merge review by an independent subagent on Opus, with a separate timing run on Sonnet. The session
that started both checked the main finding against the Ohio Revised Code itself. Every finding below is fixed in the
PR that adds this note (US V188).

- The three PRs are sound. Every hand-worked figure the reviewer recomputed matches its source and the engine:
  - Ohio: the $332 base, the joint credit's bands and the $50 senior credit per return;
  - California: the separate floors for the personal and dependent credits;

## Findings

**Medium · CONFIRMED · Ohio's joint filing credit and exemptions above $500,000 (2026 on).**
- #109 cut the credit off at $749,999 of income less exemptions, and left the exemption limit at $750,000, not modeled.
  Both figures are the 2025 rule, taken from the 2025 IT 1040 booklet.
- R.C. 5747.05(E)(2) and 5747.025 deny the credit and the exemptions at a modified AGI of $500,000 or more for taxable
  years from 2026.
- Example: a joint return with $600,000 of wages was taxed 15,347.38; the 2026 law gives 16,115.63, so the planner was
  $768 a year low.
- Fixed: `exemptionPhaseOut: { kind: 'cutoff' }` and `taxShareCredit.agiBelow`, both at $500,000 of state AGI.

**Low · CONFIRMED · existed before #109 · Ohio mapped a qualifying surviving spouse to the joint column.**
That gave a survivor with a dependent two filer exemptions and the joint filing credit, which needs a joint return
(R.C. 5747.05(E)(1)). Fixed: `qss: 'single'`.

**Low · CONFIRMED · existed before #109 · the senior deduction's phase-out read the state's start, not the federal
modified AGI.**
In a state that exempts Social Security, or includes only part of the gains, the phase-out missed that income.
- Example: DC, joint, both 67, pensions 140,000 and taxable Social Security 40,000 gave 6,449.50; the right figure is
  6,755.50.
- Fixed: `federalMagi`, used for every conforming row and DC. The other `qss: 'mfj'` mappings were left alone: those
  states follow the federal status on their own forms.

**Low · the DC senior-deduction citation.**
The notes and tests cited sec. 47-1803.04(d)(9); the allowance is (e)(6). Fixed.

**Low · test gaps.**
Added cases for:
- Ohio's credit band edges at 50,000 / 50,001 and 75,000 / 75,001;
- the $500,000 cutoff, at 499,999 and 500,000;
- the qualifying surviving spouse;
- DC in 2029;
- the senior-deduction phase-out with Social Security.

about ±3-5%.
- **TX, NY, MA:** #109 adds no cost beyond noise.
