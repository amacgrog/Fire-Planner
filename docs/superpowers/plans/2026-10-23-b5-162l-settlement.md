# Batch 5 step 1: 162(l) settlement — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every year (and cliff-plan phase) that ends holding a 162(l) deduction gets a settled return at its final draws,
priced by the reconciliation rules. The difference from what the year paid is carried into the next year's cash flow, or
into the estate after the last year.

**Architecture:** The work is in three places:
- A reconciliation mode in the ACA pricing (`src/us/core/healthcare/aca.js`), off in-year.
- A loop-level carry with its own record rules in the US year loop.
- A settlement closure in the year loop. It runs at the end of each held phase, before the phase-end block, so every
  phase decision reads the settled return.

There is one loop with no mode fork. Unheld years run exactly today's code.

**Tech Stack:** plain ESM JavaScript (esbuild bundle), Vitest 5 (Node 22.12+), Playwright scripts (`tests/*.js`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-b5-162l-settlement-design.md` (decisions 1-5 in its section 5). Read it
before starting any task: this plan names what to build, and the spec says why.

## Global Constraints

- Converged years stay byte-identical. In a run with no settling year every year is byte-identical; only E1c runs (a 162(l) deduction allowed) can change.
- No snapshot entry from before E1c moves.
- No new input (no `defaults` / `FIELDS` change).
- Timing within +5% per path (`tests/bench_runs_interleaved.js`, including `--warm-analysis`).
- The same behaviour in the standard projection, historical and Monte Carlo runs and the cliff plan's phases: one year loop, a mode switch, never a fork.
- Engine rules (CLAUDE.md): read only `ctx`; no store import; a new module-level mutable is registered in `tests/engine-purity.json` in the same commit; no allocation in a year that does not settle; never add a `continue` / `break` meant for the year loop.
- Exact comparisons only in Vitest (`toBe`, `toStrictEqual`); expected values hand-computed or two runs compared in one process; tests go through `world()`.
- Every commit ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Never `git stash`. Don't push.

## Review Focus

1. **A held year whose settled MAGI crosses the 100% floor or the Medicaid line.** A year that paid a credited premium
   keeps a credit; a Medicaid year stays $0. Pinned in Task 1 (pure) and Task 3 (`reconciliation crossing` case).
2. **A carry that tips a year across zero** (a refund larger than the need, or a balance due larger than the surplus),
   including a forced-pass year. The guardrail and `firstWithdrawal` records must equal the no-carry run's. Pinned in
   Task 2.
3. **Two-return years** (an unmarried couple, both self-employed): settled per return, the better-of choice over up to
   four combinations, and `magiHistoryA/B` from the settled pair. Pinned in Task 3 (`two returns` case).
4. **A survivor year after a held year:** the carry passes to the survivor; `tests/survivor_symmetry.js` (A dies equals
   B dies) still holds with identical self-employed partners. Pinned in Task 3, step "survivor".
5. **An unheld year next to a held one in the same run:** byte-identical records and choice for the unheld year. Pinned
   in Task 3 (`unheld year unchanged`) and Task 4 (`unheld phased year unchanged`).
